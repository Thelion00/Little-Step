Tricks that help

  • ⏸
    Pause. You don't have to answer right away. "Let me think about it" is a full answer.
  • 🔁
    Broken record. If they insist, repeat the same calm sentence. No new reasons needed.
  • ✂️
    Keep it short. Long explanations invite negotiation.
  • ↔️
    Offer something else — only if you actually want to.
  • 💙
    Notice the feeling after. Guilt fades. Self‑respect stays.

My plan

Go slow: take your usual amount, lower it by about 1 for 1–2 weeks, then step again. Small steps stick.

⚠️ If you drink every day and feel shaky, sweaty or anxious when you don't drink, do not stop suddenly. That can be dangerous. Talk to a doctor or Ingrado first so they can help you cut down safely.

What actually works

🍷 Drinking less
  • Keep a drink diary (this app does it) to see how much, when and where.
  • Pick at least 2–3 alcohol‑free days each week.
  • Never drink on an empty stomach. Eat first.
  • Have water or a soft drink between every alcoholic drink.
  • Switch to lighter drinks and sip slower.
  • Keep little or no alcohol at home.
  • Try not to drink when you're upset. Talk to someone, walk, or put music on instead.
  • Plan things to do that don't involve drinking.
🚬 Smoking less
  • Cravings come in waves and last only a few minutes. Use the Urge button.
  • Know your triggers (coffee, stress, drinking, friends) and plan for them.
  • Remove lighters and ashtrays from where you hang out.
  • Alcohol makes smoking urges stronger, so cutting both helps both.
  • Nicotine gum or patches can help with cravings. Ask a pharmacist.
  • The free Stop‑tabacco line triples the chance of success.
💨 Vaping less
  • Stretch the time between sessions: every 20 min → every 40 min → every hour.
  • Pick vape‑free places (bedroom, inside, car) and slowly add more.
  • Don't keep the vape in your hand all the time.
  • Lower the nicotine strength step by step over weeks (e.g. 20 → 12 → 6 → 3 → 0 mg).
  • Vaping a bit more right after lowering strength is normal.
  • Choose a flavour you don't love. It makes it less fun.
💙 If you slip
  • A slip is not a failure. Most people need a few tries.
  • Log it honestly, look at the trigger, try again tomorrow.
  • Remember why you started. Read the "For you" tab.
